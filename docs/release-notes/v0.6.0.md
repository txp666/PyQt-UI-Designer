## English

- The designer, live preview and exported Python applications gain 78 feedback widgets for industrial equipment, process instruments, automotive electronics, electronics DIY and embedded systems. The status word panel and digital timing chart share one 8-bit or 16-bit profile. The chart retains the latest 32 samples.
- The gallery demonstrates 84 widget instances on eight pages with 18 feedback frames. The Signals and Diagnostics page shows encoder direction, cell voltage spread, CV/CC power supply feedback and directional valve positions. [Projects and replay data](https://github.com/txp666/PyQt-UI-Designer/tree/v0.6.0/examples/industrial)
- Live charts support a separate conversion expression for each curve in preview and export. Modbus FC05 buttons accept `1` for on and `0` for off.
- The `.uidp` guide includes a working project template, task-specific references and shared property definitions. [Format guide](https://github.com/txp666/PyQt-UI-Designer/blob/v0.6.0/docs/uidp-format.md)
